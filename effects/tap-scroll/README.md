# Tap to turn

Tap the bottom third of the screen to move down, the top third to move back — reading with one
thumb without swiping. Taps on links, buttons and form fields do what they always did, and a tap
that ends a text selection is left alone.

| Option | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | 85% of the screen | 50–100% |
| Smooth scrolling | on | |
